Create site Alpha

Describe the business in the Site Creator and it writes a site spec and the prompt that builds it. Bring either back here to hand to the Kit Assistant, or paste it straight into your own agent. The site is built by the agent, not in this window.

1 · Describe the business

The Site Creator has a tailored set of questions per industry — salons, clinics, law firms, trades, venues and more — and asks straight out what real content you already have, because the build omits what you do not have rather than inventing it.

It runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

2 · Bring it back

Paste the prompt or the site spec the Site Creator produced.

What you get

A site spec — the structured description the create-site skill reads — and a prompt wrapped around it. The spec is the source of truth: anything not in it was not asked for.

Its missing list is the part that matters. Everything you did not say you have is recorded there, and the prompt tells the agent to leave those sections out. That is what stops a build inventing testimonials, prices or opening hours — which looks like less, and is the difference between a site and a demo.
